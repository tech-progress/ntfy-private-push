## Accepted cleanup policy

Standard deletion plus verified zero compute and disclosed retention satisfies the owner's cleanup policy. Stop/cancel every owned deployment and verify zero active deployments, zero running/transitional instances and zero queued work. Remove owned domains, services, verification/recovery environments, test volumes and disposable projects through standard deletion; revoke/remove any temporary remote/local SSH keys and discard local credentials. Do not delete user deployment data as qualification cleanup.

Disclose any platform-retained or pending-deletion storage, including cases where deletion completion cannot be observed. Historical `v1.0.0` source resources received standard deletion after independently verified zero compute; physical storage removal was not established. A deletion response or `Project not found` does not prove byte erasure or billing cessation. Physical storage deletion and billing-zero proof are not publication gates under the accepted policy. Do not create waiting-only resources or probe unsupported retention/admin APIs; maintainers record the bounded cleanup result and retention disclosure.
